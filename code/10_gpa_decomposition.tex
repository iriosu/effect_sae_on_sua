\maketitle

Why does GPA rise under the entrant ITT (+0.063** aggregate; event study
+0.07--0.08 at e +1..+3, -0.134 at e +4)? Two diagnostics on the entrant
classes of \texttt{8\_outcomes\_upgrade.Rmd}, run under the identical
spec. (A) A grade-year decomposition: class GPA is the mean of four
school-year grades (entry year t through t+3), so each grade-year has
its own calendar year; if the effect is the 2020--21 COVID grading
regime (flexible assessment, no repetition) hitting publicly funded
schools harder than private ones, the grade-year effects align on
\emph{calendar} years 2020--21 wherever they fall in the window; if it
is a real treatment effect, they align on \emph{treatment} and grow with
exposure. (B) An entry-class composition test: 8th-grade SIMCE (measured
before entry), share female, SIMCE coverage, and class size --
pre-determined characteristics of the entering class; treatment happens
at admission, so any movement is re-sorting, not school effects. Sample,
estimator, seed and helpers are identical to
\texttt{8\_outcomes\_upgrade.Rmd}; its entrant table
(\texttt{did\_entrants\_AM.tex}) is replicated first as the guard, which
also pins the panel to Abhijit's independent rebuild (\#15, 07-21:
identical on every column except the grad-on-time definition).
Proposal-only: results land as an issue comment, nothing enters the
draft.

\hypertarget{a.-grade-year-decomposition-of-the-gpa-effect}{%
\subsection{A. Grade-year decomposition of the GPA
effect}\label{a.-grade-year-decomposition-of-the-gpa-effect}}

The class GPA averages each student's recorded year grades over the
secondary years they attended (\texttt{gpa\_secondary}). Here it is
split into its four grade-years: \texttt{gpa\_yk} is the class mean of
members' year-k grade (calendar year = entry + k - 1), over the students
with a recorded grade that year -- so year-k grades condition on being
enrolled in year k, and treatment raises retention
(\textasciitilde+4pp); that selection is shared across calendar buckets
within a grade-year and noted when reading levels.

convention check (window mean vs stored gpa\_secondary): raw (zeros in):
cor 0.9949, mean diff -0.0111 \textbar{} zeros dropped: cor 0.8001, mean
diff +0.1481
