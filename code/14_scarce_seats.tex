\maketitle

Market means cannot move under fixed capacity -- reallocation is
zero-sum -- so \texttt{13\_first\_stage\_schools.Rmd} could not detect
seat reshuffling (the margin Arteaga et al.~show the mechanism moves at
the individual level). The compositional test: at the schools with
scarce, previously-screened seats, does WHO enters change at each wave's
onset? School sets fixed in the pre-period (entry cohorts 2012-2015,
publics): (i) REVEALED SCREENERS = top-quartile school mean entrant
SIMCE-8 (\textgreater=20 matched entrants pooled); (ii) HIGH-VA =
top-quartile va\_2016\_gazmuri. Outcomes by wave x entry cohort within
each set: mean entrant SIMCE-8 z (screening ban should pull it DOWN),
p25 (entry opening at the bottom), share below-median-ability entrants,
share out-of-commune, and cohort size (capacity response). Plus
economy-wide assortativity: corr(student z, school VA) and corr(student
z, school pre-period mean z) among public entrants -- the
vertical-sorting index that CAN move under fixed seats. SIMCE-8 gap
cohorts are blank; dense cohorts (tests 2011/13/14/15/17/19
-\textgreater{} cohorts 2012/14/15/16/18/20) carry the signal; g2018's
onset (2018) and RM's onset (2020) are directly readable, g2019's is
read at its e+1 (2020). Guard chained to
\texttt{11\_adjudication\_trajectories.Rmd}.
