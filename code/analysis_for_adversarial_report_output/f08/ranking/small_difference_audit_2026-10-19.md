# Audit of Ranking differences below five points

Scope: nonzero absolute differences of 1–4 points, admission processes 2021–2026. Exactly five points is separate; an inclusive 1–5 sensitivity is also exported. All statistics are generated by the `f08-ranking-small-differences` chunk in `analysis_for_adversarial_report.Rmd`. Production Ranking scores and manuscript tables are unchanged.

## School size

There are 430,989 records with differences of 1–4 points. Median graduating-cohort size is 94, compared with 90 for exact matches; 17.08% versus 18.50% attend cohorts smaller than 50. The median smallest historical context contains 247 versus 236 reference students. These are student-weighted measures of graduating-cohort size, not total school enrollment.

Exact Ranking matches often occur where Ranking equals NEM. The school-size gradient is therefore also exported conditional on a positive recorded Ranking bonus and separately by year, PACE-school status, and bonus status. This is a descriptive diagnostic, not a causal school-size comparison. Contexts below 30 historical students were not assigned a proxy and are outside the validation comparison.

## NEM and the student GPA

Reconstructing the four passed annual grades, rounding their mean to two decimals, and applying the year/modality-specific NEM table exactly reproduces NEM for 430,587 of 430,989 small-difference records (99.9067%). The remaining 402 cases do not replicate exactly. In the broader complete-history sample, annual NEM agreement is approximately 98.7–99.3%.

For admissions 2021, 2022, and 2026, where the stored GPA has usable decimal precision, the official table applied directly to that GPA matches recorded NEM for all 212,988 small-difference records checked. The stored GPA field for admissions 2023–2025 has lost decimal precision, so that direct stage cannot be checked in those extracts. All tables used here are strictly increasing across the 301 two-decimal GPA entries; exact NEM therefore identifies the corresponding rounded GPA, conditional on the correct year and modality.

NEM rounds the four-year average, whereas the published Ranking guide truncates context averages. Exact NEM alone does not validate that distinct rounding step, the split into school contexts, or final Ranking rounding. The existing own-average-rounding sensitivity resolves some differences but is not adopted as a rule correction.

## Historical coverage

All required reference calendar-year files are available for validated small-difference records. All three cohorts contain usable reference students for 99.133%. Nevertheless, only 15.387% retain every observed raw reference student in every context. The mean within-student pooled retention rate is 98.129% and the median minimum context retention rate is 98.980%. Missing, ambiguous, invalid, or unordered grade histories can exclude individuals even when a cohort is present.

There are still 65,047 small-difference records with all three reference cohorts present and every raw reference student retained; 64,990 of these also match NEM exactly. Thus exclusions for incomplete historical grade sequences cannot explain all discrepancies.

A stricter check leaves 26,469 small-difference records with exact NEM, one school throughout, all three cohorts, every raw reference student retained, and identical rounded and truncated student GPA. Historical-benchmark calculations, the underlying reference records, and final score precision remain to be checked even for this subset.

The denominator is the observed national grade extract, not the official DEMRE reference roster. Complete retention cannot certify that the extract includes all later grade corrections, special cases, or official school grouping assignments. The original reference cache’s 2013 start-year restriction affects 54 small-difference records; earlier raw cohorts can exist despite that restriction. This is a separate code-coverage limitation and does not explain the remaining cases.

## Interpretation

The evidence strongly supports the student’s rounded four-year GPA for the 1–4-point differences. It directs further investigation toward historical benchmark construction and the remaining Ranking-specific precision/context rules. It does not establish that one particular benchmark error explains every case.

Official rules consulted: [MINEDUC admission 2023 instruments, sections 1.1–1.2](https://acceso.mineduc.cl/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2.-INSTRUMENTOS-ACCESO-ADM-2023.pdf). The previously archived annual 2021–2026 guides and conversion tables remain the reconstruction’s source base.

Main outputs: `small_difference_group_summary.csv`, `small_difference_school_size_gradient.csv`, `small_difference_nem_stage_validation.csv`, `small_difference_historical_coverage.csv`, `small_difference_complete_reference_check.csv`, and `small_difference_existing_sensitivities.csv`. Aggregate outputs contain no student identifiers.
