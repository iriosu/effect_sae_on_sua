The eight files of Section V and Appendix C start with the line `% Generated by Section5_IV.Rmd`; after it, each is the paper's table
(the paper's tracked-change markup aside). The text numbers are the counts, shares, years and table cells that Section V and Appendix C
quote, including the footnote on the placements at the 1,045 schools (72.1% with no priority; a random number decided at least one
seat at 891 schools). Numbers written in words (such as "all four effects" or "the fourth school year"), the one million reruns of
SAE's main stage, the 20% of the top-20% indicator and the rule year the text states (from 2018) are outside the list.

## Command

From this folder (`code/finalized_design_v1/section5/`):

    Rscript -e "knitr::knit('Section5_IV.Rmd', 'output/Section5_IV.md')"

On Windows R writes the table files with CRLF line endings, on macOS and Linux with LF; compare files with
`identical(readLines(a), readLines(b))`, which reads either line ending.

## Setup

R 4.5.1 with fixest 0.14.2, dplyr 1.1.4, tidyr 1.3.1, purrr 1.0.4, tibble 3.3.0 and knitr 1.50, the versions of the run that wrote
`output/`. The notebook's `ssc(K.adj = FALSE)` needs fixest 0.13.0 or later; the paper's tables were run with fixest 0.14.2:

    install.packages(c("remotes", "dplyr", "tidyr", "purrr", "knitr"))
    remotes::install_version("fixest", "0.14.2")

## Run time

10 seconds for the command above on Windows 11.

## Standard errors

Heteroskedasticity-robust, as in Abdulkadiroglu, Angrist, Narita and Pathak (2017, "Research Design Meets Market Design").
OLS (Table III; the first stages of Tables IV and C.5; Table C.4; the offer's effect on having each score): fixest's N/(N-K) factor
and p-values from t(N-K), as Stata's `robust`. 2SLS (Tables IV, C.3 and C.5, the admission scores and the trimmed score effects): no
factor and p-values from the normal distribution, as ivreg2's `robust`. Table C.2's column (1): the weighted 2SLS of the
semiparametric estimator (weights 1/(p(1 - p)), p the chance group's offer share), standard errors without the factor. Joint tests:
F(8, N-K) from the eight regressions together, as Stata's `mvreg` followed by `test`.

## Constants

Two quantities the data files do not hold enter as constants in the setup chunk: the windows of the on-time outcomes (graduation by the
fourth school year after the application year; college outcomes by the admission process five years after it) and the last year of
the records (school year 2025; college admission and enrollment 2026), which Table C.6 and the notes of Tables C.4 and C.6 use. The
notebook checks them against the outcome columns: for the 2021 applicants each on-time outcome equals its ever outcome, and for the
2020 applicants each outcome has a child whose ever outcome is above her on-time outcome. Table IV's note gives DEMRE's 345
validation and recognition completers among its 175,869 children, from DEMRE's registration records.
