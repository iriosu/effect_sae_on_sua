\maketitle

Three descriptive checks for the exam-2023 adjudication (\#15, 07-26),
all raw student-weighted means by wave group (labeled by first treated
entry cohort g = region SAE start + 1) -- no estimation, no bootstrap.
(A) Entrant-based replication of the applied/assigned/enrolled
trajectories (the stayer-composition caveat). (B) Female-minus-male gap
trajectories: within-class gap estimates difference out class-level
calendar shocks only if the boom is gender-neutral -- the read is
whether the F-M applied gap jumps at exam 2023 in still-untreated RM.
(C) Graduation calendar check: on-time graduation for entry cohort t is
realized in calendar t+3; if the +3-4pp graduation/retention effects are
COVID promotion policy rather than SAE, untreated public groups should
jump at graduation years 2020-21 too. Sample construction identical to
\texttt{10\_gpa\_decomposition.Rmd} (guard: entrant count + six stayer
trajectory cells recomputed from students must match the dr.rds-derived
table posted on \#15).
