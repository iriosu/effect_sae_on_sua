Mechanism fingerprints for the exam-2023 boom (\#15 adjudication,
07-26). If the boom is the PAES-era eligibility machinery
(ranking/top-10\% pathway bite, floor changes on the new scale,
application cap 10 to 20, second sitting), it leaves testable marks in
the composition of applicants: (A) the marginal 2023 applicants enter
from the bottom of the score distribution; (B) high-rank / low-score
applicants -- the ranking-pathway users -- surge in 2023, more for women
(better rank, worse scores); (C) list lengths respond to the cap change;
(D) the assignment mix shifts down-market in untreated RM too, which
would reattribute the displacement/selectivity table. All raw
student-weighted descriptives on the entrant sample by wave group (g =
first treated entry cohort), no estimation. Guard: entrant count + two
trajectory cells from \texttt{11\_adjudication\_trajectories.Rmd}.
