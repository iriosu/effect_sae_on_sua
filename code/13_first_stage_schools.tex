\maketitle

The missing first stage for the meeting: did SAE change WHERE students
go at 9th-grade entry -- quality-weighted assignment, access to high-VA
schools, ability mixing across schools, private-sector avoidance,
cross-commune mobility? These outcomes are realized at entry (calendar
2012-2021), so they avoid the grading-2020, promotion-2020/21 and
PAES-2023 confounds entirely, and the within-public rollout comparison
is clean here. Read = onset alignment: does anything move at each wave's
first treated entry cohort (g2017/g2018/g2019/g2020)? All raw
student-weighted descriptives by wave group of the ENTRY SCHOOL'S REGION
(so private-school entrants belong to their region's market). Quality =
SES-adjusted 2016 school value-added (\texttt{va\_2016\_gazmuri.csv},
public schools) and 8th-grade SIMCE of entrants. Guard: entrant count +
two trajectory cells chained to
\texttt{11\_adjudication\_trajectories.Rmd}.
