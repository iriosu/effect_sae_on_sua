\maketitle

Phase 3 of issue \#14: is the female effect a supply-composition story
--- new admissions absorbed by female-typed programs? Programs are typed
by their female share among students selected in the 2014--2019
processes (before any SAE-treated cohort reaches college admission): top
tercile = female-typed, bottom = male-typed, middle (and programs with
fewer than 30 selected students) = mixed. Assigned and enrolled are then
partitioned by the destination program's type, so the three columns add
up to the baseline outcome, and the decomposition reports how much of
the female--male gap each type accounts for. Sample, estimator, seed and
helpers identical to \texttt{3\_effect\_sae\_am.Rmd}.
