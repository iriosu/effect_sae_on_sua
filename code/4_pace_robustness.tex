\title{Effect of SAE on SUA - PACE Robustness (Issue \#11)}
\author{}
\date{\vspace{-2.5em}}

\usepackage{booktabs}
\begin{document}
\maketitle

Checks whether the female-skewed SAE effects are confounded by PACE
(Programa de Acompañamiento y Acceso Efectivo), following issue \#11.
PACE guarantees seats in the centralized system to top-15\% students of
covered high-vulnerability schools; eligibility requires taking the
entrance exam and applying, so PACE moves the applied/assigned/enrolled
outcomes mechanically, and its benefits skew female (Carlana, Chiuri,
Miglino and Tincani, 2026). Three tests: (i) does PACE exposure itself
load on the SAE rollout within our design; (ii) does the gender effect
survive when PACE-track admissions are removed from the outcomes; (iii)
does it survive when ever-PACE schools are dropped. Sample, estimator,
seed and table helpers are identical to \texttt{3\_effect\_sae\_am.Rmd}.

\hypertarget{data-and-treatment}{%
\subsection{Data and treatment}\label{data-and-treatment}}

\hypertarget{pace-variables}{%
\subsection{PACE variables}\label{pace-variables}}

PACE flags come straight from the DEMRE student records, keyed to each
student's first admission process (same convention as the panel's
applied/assigned/enrolled in
\texttt{1a\_data\_processing\_primary\_secondary.Rmd}):
\texttt{is\_pace} marks students registered as PACE;
\texttt{applied\_pace}/\texttt{assigned\_pace} mark use of the PACE
application track; enrollment through PACE is an enrollment in the
program the student was selected into via the PACE track. The
\texttt{x\ PACE} outcomes remove the PACE channel from the corresponding
baseline outcome: applications only on the PACE list, assignments
through the PACE track, and enrollments following a PACE-track
assignment do not count. The DEMRE PACE flag exists from the 2017
process onward, so the 69-school pilot admissions of 2016 are not
flagged; the diagnostics below print the counts by year to check
coverage against the program's expansion (69 schools in 2014, 456 by
2016; Carlana et al., 2026) and the 2023 change in the flag's coding.

\hypertarget{heterogeneity-by-gender-removing-the-pace-channel}{%
\subsection{Heterogeneity by gender, removing the PACE
channel}\label{heterogeneity-by-gender-removing-the-pace-channel}}

Class means computed separately over the female and male students of
each class, as in the gender table of \texttt{3\_effect\_sae\_am.Rmd};
students with unknown sex are dropped and the control is the subgroup
class size. Baseline outcomes replicate the draft's gender table (a
check that this file reproduces it exactly); the \texttt{x\ PACE}
outcomes remove PACE-track applications, assignments and enrollments. If
the female effect is PACE, it shrinks toward zero here.
